Deleting your data

Privacy Terms Delete data
Last updated: 3 September 2026

There is no account to delete and no server to delete it from. Milk Ledger stores your ledger only in the app's private storage on your phone, and has no internet permission, so nothing has ever been uploaded. Deleting your data means deleting it on your device — which you can do at any time, without asking anyone.

Option 1 — Delete everything

Both of these permanently remove the entire ledger, all settings, and the app's database.

Uninstall the app

  1. Press and hold the Milk Ledger icon on your home screen or app drawer.
  2. Tap Uninstall, then confirm.

Or clear its storage, keeping the app installed

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Milk Ledger.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (on some phones, Clear data), then confirm.

The app reopens as a fresh install, starting again at the welcome screen. Exact menu names vary a little between manufacturers.

Option 2 — Delete only old records

If you want to free up space but keep your current accounts, the app can remove old history for you:

  1. Open Milk Ledger and tap the settings icon, top right.
  2. Tap Delete old records.
  3. Choose how far back to keep — 3 months, 6 months, 1 year or 2 years.
  4. Review the What will be removed panel, then tap the delete button and confirm.

This deliberately never deletes unpaid purchases or the current billing period, however old they are. Whatever it does remove is replaced by a single carried-forward opening entry, so your lifetime totals on the Profile screen stay exactly the same.

Removing your device backup

If Android's device backup was switched on, a copy of the app's data may exist in your Google account. That backup belongs to Google, not to Milk Ledger. To remove it:

  1. Open Android Settings → Google → Backup.
  2. Open the backup for your device and delete the entry for Milk Ledger, or the whole device backup.

You can also manage backups at one.google.com/settings. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete an existing device backup.

What is retained after deletion

Nothing. The developer holds no copy of your data and never did, so there is nothing left on our side to retain, anonymise or purge. Once you have deleted it on your device (and removed any device backup), it is gone.
