1. What the app is

Milk Ledger is a personal record-keeping tool for tracking daily milk purchases from a local vendor and keeping track of what you have paid. It is provided free of charge, with no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Milk Ledger is not a financial service. It does not hold money, process payments, transfer funds, extend credit or act as an intermediary between you and anyone else. It is a notebook that adds up.

2. Your data is yours, and so is keeping it

Everything you record stays on your device. The developer has no copy and no way to obtain one, which means we cannot recover your data for you if it is lost.

Your ledger can be lost if you:

  • uninstall the app,
  • clear the app's storage from Android settings,
  • use the app's Delete old records feature,
  • lose, reset or damage the device without a working device backup.

Keeping Android's device backup switched on is the practical safeguard, and it is your responsibility to do so.

3. Payments and your vendor

The app records what you tell it. It has no connection to your vendor, your bank, or any UPI system, and it cannot verify that a purchase happened or that a payment was received.

The UPI button hands off to a UPI app you already have installed; the payment itself is between you, that app and your bank. The app does not learn whether it succeeded — marking a payment in Milk Ledger is a note to yourself, not a receipt.

Any dispute about money, quantity or quality is between you and your vendor. Statements the app generates are a convenience for that conversation, not an authoritative or legal record.

4. Accuracy

The app computes totals exactly from the figures you enter, using whole paise and whole millilitres so no rounding error accumulates. It cannot know whether those figures are correct. Check the numbers before you rely on them, and before you send a statement to anyone.
